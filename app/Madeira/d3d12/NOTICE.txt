This folder holds what the app needs from Apple's Metal Shader Converter.

- libmetalirconverter.dylib (iOS build, Metal Shader Converter 4.0 beta 2,
  SHA-256 073f903be98e973ff38f4d79f2c48d61ef938754a77b1caedda79c9f05a068c2):
  Apple proprietary, tracked in this repository and distributed inside
  built Madeira packages under the Apple Metal Shader Converter agreement,
  section 2.B ("install, use, test, and distribute the Dynamic Libraries
  for the sole purpose of shader conversion"). Madeira uses it solely to
  convert DXIL shaders to Metal libraries; it is used only on Apple
  devices (agreement section 2.D). It is not covered by Madeira's GPL or
  by the Madeira Converter Exception; recipients take it under Apple's
  agreement, reproduced in full in METAL-SHADER-CONVERTER-AGREEMENT.txt.
  build/madeira-d3d12/fetch-converter.sh re-extracts and re-verifies it
  from Apple's installer package after a converter update.
- Metal Shader Converter headers (metal_irconverter*, compiled into the
  runtime): Apache License 2.0, Copyright Apple Inc.
  See LICENSE-metal-shader-converter-headers.txt. The source tree carries
  them unchanged in madeira-d3d12/third_party/metal-shader-converter.
- canary_*.dxil: Madeira's own test shaders (GPL-3.0-or-later with the
  draft additional permission in the repository's LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md).
- Madeira's own licence, the draft exception and every third-party notice
  ship in the bundle's `licenses` folder (see THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt there).
